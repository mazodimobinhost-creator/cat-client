# Code of Conduct / منشور اخلاقی مشارکت‌کنندگان

## Our Pledge / تعهد ما

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in **Cat Client** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

ما به‌عنوان اعضا، مشارکت‌کنندگان و نگه‌دارندگان پروژه **Cat Client** متعهد می‌شویم که مشارکت در این پروژه را برای همه افراد، فارغ از سطح تجربه، ملیت، زبان یا پیشینه، به تجربه‌ای محترمانه، امن و عاری از آزار تبدیل کنیم.

## Our Standards / استانداردهای رفتاری

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for users who depend on this software for open internet access
- Protecting user privacy and never sharing live private server addresses, UUIDs, or credentials

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information (including private IPs, endpoints, UUIDs, API tokens, or credentials) without permission
- Submitting intentionally malicious, backdoored, or deceptive code
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source setting

## Enforcement / ضمانت اجرا

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report incidents via GitHub's **Report abuse** or by opening an issue titled `[conduct] contact request`; a maintainer will follow up privately. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated confidentially.
